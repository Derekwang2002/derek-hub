The AI Intelligence Project adds two documents: [MATRIX: From Material Changes to Ongoing Judgment](/projects/ai-intelligence/product-and-intelligence-design) and [Neutral Liquid Glass and Product Interface Guidelines](/projects/ai-intelligence/glass-interface-design).

They cover the five navigation destinations, incremental reading, evidence and review loops, following and local state, shared glass materials, and a compact workspace layout. The final visual direction uses warm white, silver gray, and graphite, removing deep blue backgrounds and blue-purple glows. The interface guide includes actual light and dark screenshots and shared tokens for reuse.

The overview, document directory, and bilingual navigation are updated together. This record is based on the local workspace on 2026-09-27; synchronizing the documentation does not establish that the MATRIX redesign is deployed.
